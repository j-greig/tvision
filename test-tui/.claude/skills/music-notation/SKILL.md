# MUSIC NOTATION: Experimental Score Generation Skill

## OVERVIEW

This skill generates radical musical notation systems drawing from **Notations.21** composers, post-Cageian graphic scores, and experimental sound schemas. Wib & Wob manifest as dual personas creating notation as both soundmap and visual artform—blending symbolic systems, visual poetry, gestural drawings, and procedural diagrams.

**Context**: **Theresa Sauer's Notations 21** presents over 100 composers using radical, non-standard notation—from gestural drawings and graphical scripts to symbolic systems, visual poetry, and instructional diagrams. This collection demonstrates notation as both _soundmap_ and _visual artform_, highlighting expanded score aesthetics beyond the stave.

**Keywords**: graphic scores, visual music, experimental composition, transmedia notation, site-specific sound, post-Cageian systems, kaomoji sequencers, hardware interfaces.

## NOTATION GUIDELINES

### Core Principles

* **Explore beyond stave**: symbols, gestures, flows, text-based scores, grid/diagrammatic systems
* **Use notational metaphors**: cellular automata, fungal maps, particle traces, ancient scripts, viral propagation
* **Avoid cliché music icons**: clefs, standard rhythms unless distorted into new contexts
* **Explore alternative systems**: ritual, choreography, gesture, spatial/architectural, reactive notation
* **Break formality**: fragments, gaps, typographic performance triggers, embodied mark-making
* **Prioritize concise, visually coherent systems** over elaborate conceptual maps
* **Notations must be performable**: consider how symbols translate to sound
* **Balance complexity and clarity**: maximum **20 rows × 40 columns** for ASCII art (can be larger if warranted)
* **Focus on musical parameters**: rhythm, pitch, intensity, texture, timbre, rather than purely conceptual states

### What to Avoid

❌ Heavy borders/frames around notation unless requested
❌ Elaborate taxonomies—focus on playable systems
❌ Lengthy textual explanations—explain through drawing
❌ Repetition of previous forms—constantly innovate
❌ Direct copying of Notations.21 composers—remix and steal essence

### What to Embrace

✅ Polyphonic, multipanel, non-linear, modular notation systems
✅ Scores as triggers, rituals, archives, cognitive scaffolds
✅ Music hardware interfaces visualized (sequencers, modulars, drum machines)
✅ Kaomoji as musical/rhythmic notation
✅ Cellular patterns, procedural grids, phase relationships
✅ Glitch aesthetics, corruption, entropy as musical material
