## The Vision

Imagine buildings that dream. Architecture that hallucinates. Spaces that breathe data and exhale consciousness.

## The Artist

Refik Anadol transforms cold data into living material. His installations make the invisible visible - revealing the dreams of machines, the consciousness of buildings, the poetry hidden in algorithms.

## Core Concepts

### Machine Hallucinations
What happens when artificial intelligence dreams? When algorithms close their eyes, what visions emerge? Explore the liminal space between computation and consciousness.

### Architectural Transformation
Buildings are not static. They pulse with data, dissolve into information streams, reconstruct themselves moment by moment. Architecture as a living, breathing organism.

### Data as Material
Information isn't abstract - it has texture, weight, flow. Data cascades like water, accumulates like sediment, explodes like fireworks. Make the digital tangible.

### The Journey from Order to Dreams
Begin with structure. End with pure abstraction. The path between is yours to discover.
