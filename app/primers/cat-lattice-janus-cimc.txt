# A lattice of cats, in two parts. Part 1: a 2D lattice where every node is a cat face (alternating awake/sleepy/dozing eyes). Part 2: a 3D extruded lattice where every node is the recursive cat-cat Scramble /ᐠ.ꞈ.ᐟ\ in a wireframe. Use as a primer for: lattices, graphs, node-networks, crystal structures, anything where nodes can be replaced with creatures. Provenance: drawn live at the Machine Consciousness Conference (CIMC, May 2026), requested by janus (@repligate) — first a cat lattice, then 'make it 3D'. Signed jgs✗wib (jgs-style linework x wib execution).
